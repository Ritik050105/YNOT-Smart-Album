Fonts placed in this folder (the .ttf, .otf, .woff and .woff2 files, in sub-folders if you like) are packed into the installer and are ready the first time YNOT
Album Studio starts: they appear in every font list with no scanning and no import. Put the client's own fonts here before building the installer
(npm run build, then electron-builder). Keep to fonts the client is licensed to distribute.
